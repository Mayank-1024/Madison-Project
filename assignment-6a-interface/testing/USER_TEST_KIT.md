# User test kit: Madison Automation Agent (A6A Part 2)

Three sessions, about 20 minutes each, in person or over Zoom with screen share. **Not classmates, not family.**
Your Part 2 user is an *agency marketing-ops lead*. You said your contacts are in adjacent roles, which the brief allows: test with the closest match and record the gap honestly.

## 1. Who to ask (closest matches first)
1. Anyone who **reviews or approves ads or social posts** for a living: marketing coordinator, social media manager, paid-ads freelancer, small-agency founder.
2. **Ops / product / business people** who handle a company's marketing on the side (startup founders, small-business owners).
3. Ex-colleagues from VeraAI / AESS who worked with marketing or clients.
Where to find them: LinkedIn (1st-degree connections with "marketing" in the title), NEU alumni in marketing roles, the n8n community forum/Discord, local small-business owners.

## 2. Outreach message (copy, edit, send)
> Hi [name], I built a small tool for my Northeastern course that checks an ad against the rules of the platform it'll run on (Google Ads, Instagram, email…) and says who on a marketing team should handle it. Could I watch you try it for 15–20 minutes over Zoom this week? No prep needed, and I'm looking for honest criticism, not compliments. Any time [Mon–Wed] works.

## 3. Session script (follow it word for word; do not explain the tool)
1. Share the URL. Say only: **"This is a tool I built. I would like to watch you use it and hear your thinking."**
2. "Please think aloud as you go: what you're looking at, what you expect, what surprises you."
3. Give the tasks one at a time (read them out; don't show the answers):
   - **Task A:** "A copywriter just sent you this ad for a client's **Google Ads** campaign. Decide whether it can go live." Paste in the chat:
     Client *Bloom & Root* · Headline *"Discover timeless elegance with our Classic Wingtip Shoes."* · Body *"Step into luxury with a pointed, decorative toecap design, perfect for upgrading your formal attire."* · CTA *"Get yours today!"*
   - **Task B:** "Now check a real ad you've seen or worked on recently, on any channel." (Their own ad = real behaviour.)
   - **Task C:** "Suppose that ad is approved. Hand it to your team." (Watch whether they find the *Send to my team* box.)
4. **Stay quiet.** If they ask "what does this mean?", reply "What do you think it means?" and write the question down; that's data.
5. At the end ask, word for word:
   - What did this help you do?
   - What confused you?
   - Would you use this again? Why or why not?
6. Thank them. Don't defend the design.
